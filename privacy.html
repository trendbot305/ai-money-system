Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 September 2026

1. Information we process

Depending on the features you use, AI Money System may process account information, creator content and technical information needed to operate the service.

2. Connected social accounts

When you connect a social platform such as TikTok, we may process platform account identifiers, authorization tokens, publishing information, public video identifiers and public performance metrics that you have authorized the platform to provide.

3. TikTok permissions and analytics

For TikTok integrations, the service may use permissions such as user.info.basic to identify the connected account, video.publish to publish creator-authorized videos, and video.list to read the authenticated creator's public videos and available public performance fields such as views, likes, comments and shares. These permissions are used only to provide the connected features requested by the creator.

4. How information is used

Information is used to operate the service, provide requested integrations, display analytics, improve creator content recommendations, maintain security and troubleshoot issues.

5. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be sent to third-party platforms when necessary to provide a feature you have requested, such as publishing content to or retrieving authorized public analytics from a connected social account.

6. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, subject to operational, legal and security requirements.

8. Your choices

You may disconnect supported social accounts or revoke platform permissions through the relevant platform. You may also request information about data associated with your use of the service through the contact method provided with your account or service setup.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated as the service develops. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the contact method provided with your account or service setup.
