AI Money System

A creator workflow for turning research and ideas into short-form social content.

What it does

AI Money System helps creators organize content opportunities, research topics, create content plans, generate short-form videos, run quality checks and prepare content for distribution to supported social platforms.

Social publishing

Where a platform integration is enabled, a creator explicitly connects and authorizes their account. The system then uses the permissions granted by that creator to perform supported publishing actions.

Creator control

Creators remain responsible for reviewing their content, platform settings and publishing decisions. Platform-specific limits, approvals and privacy settings apply.
